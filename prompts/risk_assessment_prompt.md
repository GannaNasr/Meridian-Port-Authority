# Container Risk Assessment Prompt

## Purpose
Analyze container information and evaluate the risk level before performing any operation.

## Instructions

Review the following information:

- Container status.
- Hazmat status.
- Customs hold status.
- Carrier status.
- Previous transactions.

Provide:

- Risk level.
- Main risk factors.
- Required approvals.
- Recommended action.

## Output Format

Risk Level:
Low / Medium / High

Risk Factors:
List the reasons affecting the risk.

Required Approvals:
Mention any required human approvals.

Recommendation:
Explain the next recommended step.
